EDIT / THE MIND

Generate sentences from 18 grammatical patterns: questions, negation, past and future forms, conditional clauses, relatives and contrasts. Twist GRAMMAR and DAMAGE to rewrite the syntax or begin losing words. REC remembers a noun and RECALL brings it back.

FX / THE MOUTH

A compact formant-synthesis robot that says the words: gliding formants, hisses and stops, a pitch line over each sentence. Bend pitch, rate, tone, breath and stutter. Press piano keys to articulate successive words at different pitches.

Start in five seconds

Every white key has a word and says a phrase about it. The black key beside it says the same word another way: a question, a “not”, a “was”. SEQ gives the key a new word, REC remembers it, PLAY babbles about it. SEL steps through the knobs: voice, mouth, words. Hold GLO for a guide on the screen.

BabbleBeat, STRATA and PALINDROME are independent firmwares. The browser emulator uses the same C engine as the hardware target. Hardware sound and flash safety are not yet validated.
