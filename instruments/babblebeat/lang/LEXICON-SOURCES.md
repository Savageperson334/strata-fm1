# Where BabbleBeat's words come from

`babble_words.h` holds about 2,900 words: 1,796 nouns in fourteen worlds, 512 adjectives and 590 verbs. It is generated by `../tools/gen_lexicon.py` and committed, so building the firmware needs neither source below.

| What | From |
| --- | --- |
| The first four worlds (MACHINE, DREAM, BODY, GOTHIC), 12 adjectives and 16 verbs | BabbleBeat's own, written by hand |
| How every other word is said | The CMU Pronouncing Dictionary, each sound respelled in the letters BabbleBeat's voice uses |
| What kind of word it is, whether a verb takes an object, which world a noun is in, the order of a world's nouns, and how common a word is | Princeton WordNet 3.0 |

The generator keeps a word only if it is a plain lowercase word of three to nine letters (verbs: eight), is commoner as that kind of word than as another, and can be said with the sounds the voice has. It drops rude and cruel words, clinical ones, and words about kinds of people. The list was sampled, not read word by word: tell us about any word that should go, and add it to `BLOCK` in the generator.
