# Where BabbleBeat's words come from

`babble_words.h` holds about 9,800 words: 6,807 nouns in twenty-one worlds, 1,412 adjectives, 1,183 verbs, 350 adverbs and 30 prepositions. It is generated by `../tools/gen_lexicon.py` and committed, so building the firmware needs neither source below.

| What | From |
| --- | --- |
| The first four worlds (MACHINE, DREAM, BODY, GOTHIC), 12 adjectives and 16 verbs | BabbleBeat's own, written by hand |
| How every other word is said | The CMU Pronouncing Dictionary, each sound respelled in the letters BabbleBeat's voice uses |
| What kind of word it is, whether a verb takes an object, which worlds a verb suits, which world a noun is in, the order of a world's nouns, and how common a word is | Princeton WordNet 3.0 |

The generator keeps a word only if it is a plain lowercase word of three to nine letters (verbs: eight), is at least as common as that kind of word as any other, and can be said with the sounds the voice has ("zh" is said as "z"). It takes the commonest of each world up to a limit, and is generous: rare and odd words are in. It still drops rude and cruel words and clinical ones. The list was sampled, not read word by word: tell us about any word that should go, and add it to `BLOCK` in the generator.
