# BabbleBeat

Third standalone M-VAVE FM-1 firmware in the STRATA / PALINDROME repository.

The first version is deliberately focused on a **sentence grammar generator
and mutator**, with a small formant-synthesis voice. It is a new instrument,
not a port of the old Python WordNet/Dracula program. The tiny thematic
lexicons echo that program's imagery, while the grammar and interface are
written for the FM-1. No source or DSP from CHIMERA has been copied.

The language engine is deterministic, heap-free C: templates assemble
sentences from nouns, inflected verbs, adjectives, and function words.
Tokens carry their grammatical role so mutations can operate on nouns
and adjectives without destroying the pattern, or can repeat/delete
tokens when DAMAGE is turned up. GRAMMAR chooses, for every kind of phrase,
one of five forms from bare to nested. REC remembers one noun and RECALL
controls its future appearance.

The voice plays compact hand-written phoneme codes through a small formant
synthesizer in the manner of Klatt's: a glottal pulse and breath noise through
four formant resonators in cascade, shaped noise for hisses and bursts, and
formants that glide from one sound to the next. It is robotic on purpose; it
is not general-purpose TTS and says only what the lexicon spells.

The lexicon is about 9,800 words in twenty-one worlds, with verbs that lean
toward the kind of noun they are about. The first four worlds are
hand-written; the rest is generated by `tools/gen_lexicon.py` from the CMU
Pronouncing Dictionary and WordNet 3.0 into `lang/babble_words.h`
([sources and notices](lang/LEXICON-SOURCES.md)).

## Build / simulate

After fetching the fm1-base submodule and its prerequisites:

~~~sh
cc -O2 -Iinstruments/babblebeat/lang \
  instruments/babblebeat/tests/lang.c instruments/babblebeat/lang/babble_lang.c -o /tmp/babble-lang
/tmp/babble-lang

cc -O2 -Ibase/include -Iinstruments/babblebeat/lang -Iinstruments/babblebeat/dsp \
  instruments/babblebeat/tests/voice.c instruments/babblebeat/lang/babble_lang.c \
  instruments/babblebeat/dsp/babble_voice.c -lm -o /tmp/babble-voice
/tmp/babble-voice

python3 base/tools/build.py instruments/babblebeat --host
mkdir -p instruments/babblebeat/build/shots
instruments/babblebeat/build/host/babblebeat_host \
  instruments/babblebeat/tests/basic.txt instruments/babblebeat/build/shots
python3 base/tools/build.py instruments/babblebeat --web
~~~

The hardware build also needs the JieLi SDK/toolchain. The package identity is `FM-1_60`.

## Release

A change under `instruments/babblebeat/` on `main` runs `.github/workflows/babblebeat.yml`: the tests, the browser
emulator, and, when `VERSION` names a version with no `babblebeat-vX.Y` tag yet, the firmware built with the shared
FM-1 builder, a signed prerelease, and the installer, manual and emulator published under `/babblebeat/`. The
hardware has not been validated: the firmware is experimental.
