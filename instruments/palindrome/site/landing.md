# PALINDROME

**MORPH makes a sound. REC catches it in one of three rings. The rings are scanned.**

![PALINDROME's three rings](manual/img/rings.png "SCAN: three rings")

## Touch and go

- **Tap a key:** MORPH strikes a note one ring long (1.49 s), ring 1 catches it, and it starts going round.
- **SCAN (EDIT):** POSITION scrubs, SIZE and DENSITY make grains or the plain recording, PITCH transposes, SPEED runs it backward, freezes it or doubles it, SPREAD scatters it.
- **Keys:** the 16 white keys are 16 places round the ring, as on a pocket sampler; SEL makes them pitches instead.
- **MORPH (FX):** a note's hidden envelopes carry it to its mirror image and back over the ring (MOVE); SHAPE is its loudness.
- **Rings:** an empty ring catches the next key; hold REC and tap a key to catch over a full one; tap REC on SCAN to resample the others into it.
- **ENV** freezes the ring, **LFO** reverses it, **HOME** held empties it.
- **SELECT:** the ring. **GLO** (hold): the guide on the screen.
